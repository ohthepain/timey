## Original request

Show Retro follow-ups that failed to publish to the shared queue

The queue cutover made local capture and shared publication separate operations. In scripts/factory-ui/shared-issue-queue.mjs, a queue publication failure is returned as a saved status. In scripts/factory-ui/flow-service.mjs, the closure path treats a returned capture view as success, sets filedRetro and moves to Cleanup without checking or surfacing the proposal's publication outcome on the parent. A proposal may be visible on the originating runner while a second runner cannot see it. This is a source finding; no live failure was forced.

## Requested proof

In a disposable shared-queue fixture, fail publication for one of two recommendations and complete or hold the other for review. On the parent issue, distinguish local filing, waiting for review and failed publication; link to each proposal and expose the existing retry route. Retry the failed publication with the same proposal IDs and verify there are no duplicates or lost approval decisions. Preserve the separate closure and cleanup rules.
